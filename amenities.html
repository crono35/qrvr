Comfortable Desert Camping

Amenities That Make Your Stay Easy

Quartzsite RV Resort is designed for travelers who want plenty of room, dependable hookups, useful on-site amenities, and a comfortable place to settle in—whether you're staying a few nights or spending the season.

Room to Settle In

Spacious Sites for Big Rigs and Long Stays

Our 27 oversized RV sites can accommodate everything from Class B and Class C motorhomes to large Class A coaches and fifth wheels up to 45 feet. Select sites can accommodate luxury motor coaches up to approximately 52 feet.

With full hookups, concrete pads, room for two vehicles, and utilities included in our daily rate category, you can arrive, get comfortable, and start enjoying Quartzsite.

Oversized Full-Hookup Sites

Large RV sites with 30- and 50-amp electric service, water, sewer, and room for larger rigs.

Concrete Pads

Level concrete pads provide a cleaner, more comfortable setup for your RV and outdoor space.

Parking for Two Vehicles

Each site offers convenient space for your RV plus additional vehicle parking.

Utilities Included

Water, sewer, garbage service, and electricity are included in all rates.

Clubhouse & Game Room

Relax indoors with big-screen TVs, a pool table, shuffleboard, and space to gather with friends.

Wi-Fi Available

Wi-Fi is available on-site for general browsing and everyday use. Speeds and bandwidth may be limited, especially during busy periods.

Shower Facilities

On-site shower facilities give guests an extra option for freshening up during their stay.

On-Site Laundry

Convenient laundry facilities make extended and seasonal stays easier.

Indoor Kitchen

A shared indoor kitchen provides extra space for preparing meals and gathering with other guests.

Outdoor Cooking & Fire Pit

Enjoy our BBQ pit, smoker, and fire pit for relaxed evenings and outdoor meals.
