Guest Information

Policies & Park Rules

These policies help keep Quartzsite RV Resort safe, comfortable, and enjoyable for everyone. Please review them before your stay.

Cancellation & Refund Policy

A minimum of 48 hours' cancellation notice is required to receive a refund of your deposit, less a $10 cancellation fee.

Please notify the office by 4:00 p.m. two days before your scheduled arrival. Text-message cancellations are not accepted.

  • No refunds are issued for reservations during the month of January.
  • Once you have checked in, the reservation is non-refundable.
  • For long-term stays, unused days are not refunded if you choose to leave early.

Questions about a cancellation or refund? Please call the office at (626) 244-5276.

Check-In, Check-Out & Payment

  • Check-in: 12:00 p.m.
  • Check-out: 11:00 a.m.
  • Earlier check-in may be possible if your site is unoccupied and ready.
  • Late check-out may be available with manager approval, depending on the next guest's arrival.
  • All rent is due upon check-in.
  • A deposit is due when a reservation is accepted, with the remaining balance due prior to or upon arrival as required by the reservation.

Quiet Hours & Guest Conduct

  • Quiet hours are 10:00 p.m. to 7:00 a.m.
  • No generators may be used during quiet hours.
  • Loud music is not permitted at any time.
  • Disorderly, threatening, abusive, disruptive, obscene, or unlawful behavior will not be tolerated.
  • Guests may be removed from the property for serious or repeated rule violations.
  • Guests are expected to avoid conduct that creates a nuisance or unreasonable annoyance for neighboring sites.

Vehicles, Parking & Speed

  • The campground speed limit is 5 mph.
  • All vehicles and trailers must fit within your assigned site unless additional parking has been arranged with the office.
  • Do not block neighboring sites or roadways.
  • Vehicle washing and vehicle servicing are not permitted in the park.
  • Electric vehicles may not be charged from RV site electrical pedestals.
  • Unauthorized EV charging may result in a $150 fee.

RV Hookups & Site Safety

  • Guests are responsible for safe and proper connections to electric, water, and sewer service.
  • Connections must be kept leak-free and in safe operating condition.
  • Sewer hoses must be properly secured using an appropriate connector and seal.
  • Sewer caps must be returned to the inlet at check-out. Missing caps may result in a $10 replacement charge.
  • Do not alter campground electrical, water, or sewer connections.
  • Your RV and site should be kept clear enough to allow the rig to be moved promptly in an emergency.

Pets

  • Pets are welcome, subject to management approval.
  • Pets must be kept on a leash and may not be left unattended outdoors.
  • Pets should be registered with the office, and vaccination records must be available upon request.
  • Pet waste must be picked up immediately and disposed of properly.
  • Excessive barking, uncontrolled behavior, or aggressive behavior is not permitted.
  • Pets are not permitted inside public buildings or amenity areas where posted or restricted.
  • Please be especially cautious with pets outdoors; coyotes are present in the Quartzsite area.

Fires, Smoking & Prohibited Items

  • Fires must be contained in an above-ground fire pit and covered by a grate or mesh.
  • Do not burn trash or gather firewood from the surrounding desert.
  • Fireworks are prohibited anywhere in the park.
  • Smoking is not permitted inside resort buildings and is strictly prohibited near the propane tank.
  • Cigarette butts must be disposed of properly and may not be thrown on the ground.
  • Brandishing a firearm is prohibited.

Site Appearance & RV Standards

RVs and campsites should be maintained in a clean, safe, and presentable condition. The resort does not generally impose an RV age limit, but vehicles should be operable and in reasonably good condition.

  • Doors, panels, windows, awnings, and exterior components should be intact and maintained.
  • Tarps may not be used as permanent weatherproofing or as a substitute for proper RV repairs.
  • Temporary shade structures may be used during a reservation; larger or semi-permanent structures require management approval.
  • Indoor household furniture and appliances should not be placed outdoors at the campsite.
  • Keep the site neat, uncluttered, and free of fire hazards.
  • Clotheslines should not be visible at the site.

Trash, Common Areas & Amenities

  • Place tied trash bags in the dumpsters and break down cardboard boxes before disposal.
  • Please clean up after yourself when using the clubhouse and other common areas.
  • Wi-Fi is available as a guest amenity, but signal strength and bandwidth may vary by site and during busy periods.
  • Please do not leave food outdoors where it may attract wildlife.

Responsibility for Property & Damage

Guests are responsible for their personal property. Quartzsite RV Resort LLC is not responsible for lost, stolen, or damaged personal items.

Guests are responsible for damage to resort property caused by themselves, members of their party, visitors, or pets, including damage to utility connections, landscaping, facilities, and other property.

Emergency

In the event of an emergency inside the park, call 911.

These website policies are provided as a convenient summary of current park rules and reservation terms. Additional terms may apply to your reservation through Campspot or at check-in.
